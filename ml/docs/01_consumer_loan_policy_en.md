# Mtsvane Bank — Consumer Loan Policy (training document, fictional bank)

Version 4.2 · Effective 1 March 2026 · Owner: Retail Credit Risk

## 1. Who can apply
1.1 Applicants must be at least 20 years old and no older than 70 at the end of the loan term.
1.2 Applicants must be tax residents of Georgia and hold a valid Georgian ID card or residence permit.
1.3 Freelancers and self-employed applicants are eligible. They must provide at least 6 months of bank statements showing income. Income from at least 4 of the last 6 months is required.

## 2. Loan amounts and terms
2.1 Unsecured consumer loans: 500 GEL to 30,000 GEL.
2.2 Terms: 6 to 60 months.
2.3 Loans to individuals are issued in GEL. Loans in foreign currency are only available when the applicant's income is in the same currency.

## 3. Affordability (payment-to-income)
3.1 The bank follows the National Bank of Georgia responsible lending rules. The payment-to-income ratio (PTI) is all monthly loan payments, including the new loan, divided by verified net monthly income.
3.2 For GEL loans: if net monthly income is below 1,500 GEL, PTI may not exceed 25%. If income is 1,500 GEL or more, PTI may not exceed 50%.
3.3 For loans in foreign currency the limits are 20% and 30% respectively.
3.4 The income threshold of 1,500 GEL rises to 2,000 GEL from 1 February 2027 and to 2,500 GEL from 1 September 2027.

## 4. Credit decision
4.1 Every application is scored by the bank's credit model, which estimates the probability of default (PD) within 12 months.
4.2 PD below 6%: automatic approval if affordability rules are met. PD from 6% to 12%: manual review by a credit analyst. PD above 12%: decline.
4.3 A declined applicant receives the main reasons for the decision (reason codes) in plain language and may request a manual review within 30 days.
4.4 The credit model does not use gender, ethnicity, religion or health data.

## 5. Interest and fees
5.1 Annual interest rate for GEL consumer loans: 14% to 22%, depending on risk band.
5.2 Loan issuance fee: 1% of the loan amount, minimum 30 GEL.
5.3 Early repayment is allowed at any time. Fee for early repayment: 0% for loans under 24 months, up to 1% of the repaid amount otherwise.
